TechNova 2026 FAQ

Quick answers about teams, domains, registration, tools, the schedule and prizes. Each answer links to the page that has the full details.

Question groups

TechNova 2026 is a fictional class prototype. Where something is not decided, the answer says so. The rules page is the official source if anything looks different.

Eligibility and team formation

Who can participate?

Only current college or university students can participate.

Proof of student status will be required, but the method is not decided yet. See Rules & Eligibility.

Is prior hackathon experience required?

No hackathon experience is required in the proposed format.

Mentor sessions and checkpoints are planned to help newer teams. This is a proposal, not a final rule.

What is the maximum team size?

A team can have up to six members, including the leader.

See team formation rules.

Can one student join multiple teams?

No, each student can belong to only one team.

Registering the same student twice would break this rule. See the rules.

Can a team form during the event?

This has not been decided.

For now, plan to register a full team in advance through the registration page. Any change will be posted on Updates.

Domains and problem statements

What domains are available?

The domains are Sustainability, Education Technology, Healthcare and Finance, plus a proposed Sponsor Tracks option.

Browse examples on Domains & Problem Statements.

Can a team select a sponsor track?

Not yet, because no sponsors are confirmed.

Sponsor tracks are a proposed format. See Domains and Prizes & Sponsors.

Can a team propose its own idea?

Yes, within the supported domains, since the event is open-domain.

Guided problem statements are there to help, not to limit you. See Domains.

Is an idea pitch mandatory?

No, the idea pitch on the registration form is optional.

You can register without one.

Registration

Is registration free?

Yes, registration is free.

See the registration page.

What information is required?

The demo asks for a team name, leader, member count, member details and a first-choice domain.

The idea pitch is optional. The form uses made-up data only.

What student-identification details are needed?

This has not been decided.

The demo only accepts sample IDs like DEMO-0001. Never enter a real student ID. Check the rules for updates.

Is the registration form real or a demonstration?

It is a demonstration.

It does not register anyone and stores nothing. TechNova 2026 is a fictional class project.

Tools and preparation

What programming languages or tools may be used?

The draft sets no language or tool list.

Any limits will be published on the rules page.

Can teams use open-source libraries?

Yes, as long as you credit them.

See originality and attribution.

How should third-party code and datasets be attributed?

Record each item's name, source and licence in your project credits.

The exact credit format will be set with the submission format. See the rules.

What should participants prepare before arriving?

Form a team, choose a preferred domain and read the rules.

Logistics such as equipment are not set yet. See Register and Rules.

Schedule and submission

When does the event begin?

Exact dates are not confirmed.

The draft uses a Friday-to-Sunday format with an evening start. See the schedule.

When is the final submission deadline?

The proposed deadline is 11:30 AM on Sunday.

See submission rules. The advertised 48 hours versus about 39 building hours is flagged on the schedule.

Are mentorship sessions and checkpoints part of the schedule?

Yes, the draft includes mentor sessions and checkpoints, mainly on Saturday.

See the schedule.

Where can participants see the latest timetable?

The schedule page always holds the latest timetable.

Changes are also logged on Updates & Archive.

Prizes and judging

What award categories are proposed?

Six are proposed: Overall Winner, First and Second Runner-Up, Best Innovation, Best UI/UX and Best Social Impact.

The proposed pool is ₹1,20,000. See Prizes & Sponsors. These are prototype figures, not a real commitment.

How will projects be evaluated?

Judging criteria have not been defined yet.

See judging and evaluation.

Are sponsor-provided prizes confirmed?

No, no sponsors or sponsor prizes are confirmed.

See Prizes & Sponsors.

Practical information

Where is the venue?

The venue is to be announced, and this fictional event has no real venue.

Updates would appear on Updates.

Is accessibility information available?

Not yet.

The prototype has no monitored support channel. See Contact.

How can sponsors or judges make contact?

Use the contact page.

It is a demonstration form, so no messages are read.

Where will announcements and updates appear?

On the Updates & Archive page.

Entries there are sample entries in this prototype.
