TechNova 2026 Schedule
48-hour build · Friday to Sunday

From the first idea to a judged prototype.

The proposed programme moves from check-in and orientation to challenge selection, development, mentorship, submission and final evaluation. Times and rooms below form a coherent planning schedule for this academic prototype; they are not a confirmed live-event announcement.

Event timetable

Room assignments use the listed P-block and NLH classrooms. The schedule is arranged so the proposal round begins two hours after the official build kickoff and the submission deadline closes the 48-hour build window.

Judging criteria & weightage

The proposed rubric assigns a percentage weight to each evaluation area, giving reviewers a consistent basis for assessing the problem, the solution and the quality of the demonstration.

Problem understanding & relevance
Clarity of the problem, target users and evidence of need.
15%
Innovation & originality
Distinctiveness of the idea and quality of the approach.
20%
Technical implementation
Working functionality, technical depth and integration.
25%
Usability & user experience
Ease of use, accessibility and clarity of the user journey.
15%
Impact, feasibility & responsible design
Potential value, realistic scope and responsible development.
15%
Presentation & responses
Demo quality, communication and responses to reviewers.
10%
TOTAL WEIGHTAGE100%
