Rules & Eligibility

Who can join TechNova 2026, how teams work, what counts as original work and when the final submission is due. This is the one official source for the rules; other pages link here.

Who can participate

Who is eligible?

Only current college or university students can take part. Every team member must be a current student.

How will student status be verified?

Verification is required, but the proof has not been chosen yet To be confirmed. This page will name the accepted proof once the process is defined. The registration demo asks for made-up sample IDs only, never real ones.

Team formation

How big can a team be?

A team has up to six members, including the team leader.

Can I join more than one team?

No. Each student may belong to one team only.

How is a team shown at registration?

The team leader registers the team and lists every member on the registration form.

Can teams form during the event?

Not decided To be confirmed. Until then, plan to register a complete team in advance.

Originality and attribution

Does the project have to be original?

Yes. Your project must be your team's own work, or clearly credit anything it borrows.

How are third-party code, datasets, APIs, libraries and assets handled?

You may use them if you credit them. For each one, record its name, where it came from and its licence. The credit format will be set with the submission format To be confirmed.

Do I have to disclose AI tools?

The draft does not decide this yet To be confirmed. If disclosure becomes required, it will be listed here.

Participation requirements

  • Register your team through the registration page and pick a preferred domain.
  • Attend check-in and orientation on Friday Proposed.
  • Take part in mentor sessions and checkpoints shown on the schedule.
  • Deliverables, presentation requirements and technical limits are not set yet To be confirmed.

Conduct

  • Work respectfully with teammates, mentors, organizers and other teams.
  • Do not plagiarise or misrepresent who built what.
  • Use data, software and third-party assets only in ways their licences allow.
  • Disqualification rules are not defined yet To be confirmed.

Submission rules

When is the deadline?

Final submissions are due at 11:30 AM on Sunday In planning draft. The draft timetable gives about 39 building hours from a Friday 8:30 PM start, so the advertised 48 hours is flagged for confirmation on the schedule.

How do I submit?

The method and format are not decided To be confirmed.

What happens to late or incomplete work?

Not decided To be confirmed.

Judging and evaluation

Judging criteria have not been defined To be confirmed, and no judges are named. The proposed award categories are on Prizes & Sponsors.
